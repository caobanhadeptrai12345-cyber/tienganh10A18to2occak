Volunteer activities, including support for nursing homes

2

What is volunteering?

Volunteering means giving time and effort to help others without expecting money in return. Small acts of help can make a big difference in a community.

3

Ways to help people

  • Visiting and keeping company with lonely people
  • Tutoring children or helping them with homework
  • Donating food, clothes, or books to those in need
  • Cleaning up parks, streets, and public places
4

Volunteering at a nursing home

A nursing home is where older people live and receive daily care. Volunteers can:

  • Chat and listen to stories
  • Play games, read, or sing together
  • Help with light activities like gardening or crafts
5

How to be a good volunteer

  • Be polite, patient, and respectful
  • Ask staff what you are allowed to do
  • Respect each person's privacy and choices
  • Keep your promises and arrive on time
6

Benefits

  • For older people: less loneliness, more joy, and a sense of connection
  • For volunteers: new skills, empathy, and confidence
  • For the community: stronger bonds between generations
