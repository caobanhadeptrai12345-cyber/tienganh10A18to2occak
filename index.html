UNIT 4 · FOR A BETTER COMMUNITY

Helping People in Our Community

Volunteer activities, including support for nursing homes

01 · DEFINITION

What is volunteering?

Volunteering means giving time and effort to help others without expecting money in return.

Small acts of help, when many people do them together, can make a big difference in a community.

02 · ACTIVITIES

Ways to help people

Visit and keep company

Spend time with people who feel lonely.

Tutor children

Help children who need support with homework.

Donate

Share food, clothes, or books with people in need.

Clean public spaces

Clean up parks, streets, and public places.

03 · NURSING HOME

Volunteering at a nursing home

A nursing home is where older people live and receive daily care. Volunteers can:

  • Chat and listen to stories
  • Play games, read, or sing together
  • Help with light activities like gardening or crafts
04 · GOOD MANNERS

How to be a good volunteer

1

Be polite, patient, and respectful

2

Ask staff what you are allowed to do

3

Respect each person's privacy and choices

4

Keep your promises and arrive on time

05 · BENEFITS

Why volunteering matters

For older people

Less loneliness, more joy, and a sense of connection.

For volunteers

New skills, empathy, and confidence.

For the community

Stronger bonds between generations.
