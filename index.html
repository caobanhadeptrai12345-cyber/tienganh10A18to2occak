UNIT 4 · FOR A BETTER COMMUNITY

Helping People in Our Community

Volunteer activities, including support for nursing homes

01 · DEFINITION

What is volunteering?

Volunteering means giving time and effort to help others without expecting money in return.

Small acts of help, when many people do them together, can make a big difference in a community.

02 · ACTIVITIES

Ways to help people

  • Visiting and keeping company with lonely people
  • Tutoring children or helping them with homework
  • Donating food, clothes, or books to those in need
  • Cleaning up parks, streets, and public places
